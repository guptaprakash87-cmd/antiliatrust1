WHO WE ARE

Rescuing the Forgotten,
Restoring Dignity

Antilia Trust is dedicated to the rehabilitation of beggars and non-livelihood communities — providing shelter, nutrition, skill training, healthcare and a path back to a dignified life across Karnataka and beyond.

🎯

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate and reintegrate beggars, destitute persons and non-livelihood communities — restoring their dignity through shelter, food, healthcare, vocational training and social inclusion programs across India.

🌟

Our Vision

An India where no human being has to beg for survival — where every destitute person is rescued, supported and given the skills and opportunity to live with self-reliance, dignity and belonging in society.
