ABOUT ANTILIATRUST

Restoring Lives, Rebuilding Futures

Antilia Trust is dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation, and reintegration of beggars and destitute persons across Karnataka and India.

Founded by M/S PRATIMA KUMARI, we believe that every human being deserves a life of dignity. Through our shelter homes, nutrition drives, and skill training programs, we provide the tools and support needed for individuals to transition from the streets to self-reliance.
